## Manifold Markets CPMM math

`server/prediction-market.mjs` adapts the fee-free constant-product market maker probability and share calculations from [`manifoldmarkets/manifold/common/src/calculate-cpmm.ts`](https://github.com/manifoldmarkets/manifold/blob/main/common/src/calculate-cpmm.ts). It is used only for simulated event-probability quotes.
